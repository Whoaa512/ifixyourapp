## 1. Services Included

During the retainer period, Consultant will provide:

- Bug fixes and patch deployments
- Dependency updates and security patches
- Application monitoring and alerting response
- Minor feature additions (< 4 hours each)
- Code review and architecture guidance
- Priority scheduling over non-retainer clients

## 3. Minimum Commitment

This agreement has a **3-month minimum term** starting from the effective date. After the minimum term, either party may cancel with 30 days written notice (email is fine).

## 4. Response Time SLAs

| Severity | Description | Response Time |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Critical** | App is down or losing data | 4 hours (business hours) |
| **High** | Major feature broken, users impacted | 8 business hours |
| **Medium** | Bug affecting some users, workaround exists | 1 business day |
| **Low** | Minor issue, cosmetic, or improvement | 3 business days |

"Response time" means acknowledgment and initial assessment, not necessarily resolution. Complex issues get a timeline estimate after triage.

## 5. Out-of-Scope Work

The following are **not included** in the retainer and will be quoted separately:

- New feature development (> 4 hours)
- Full application rebuilds or rewrites
- Infrastructure migrations
- Third-party integrations requiring new vendor accounts
- Work requiring technologies outside the current stack

If a request exceeds retainer scope, we'll let you know before proceeding and provide a separate proposal.

## 6. How It Works

1. Submit requests via [EMAIL / SLACK / GITHUB ISSUES — pick one].
2. We acknowledge and assign severity per the SLA table.
3. Work is performed and deployed (or submitted for review, per your preference).
4. Monthly summary sent with hours used, work completed, and any recommendations.

## 7. Overage

If included hours are exhausted before month-end:

- We notify you when 80% of hours are used.
- Additional work billed at $150/hour.
- You may decline additional work and defer to next month.
- Overage invoiced with the following month's retainer.

## 8. IP Ownership

All code produced under this retainer is owned by Client upon payment. Same terms as our standard [Terms of Service](https://ifixyour.app/terms.html).

## 9. Confidentiality

Same as our standard Terms of Service. We never share your code or data. Period.

## 10. Cancellation

- **During minimum term:** Remaining months are due upon cancellation.
- **After minimum term:** 30 days written notice. Final month is prorated if applicable.
- **By Consultant:** 30 days notice with transition support to hand off cleanly.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Total liability under this agreement is limited to fees paid in the preceding 3 months.
