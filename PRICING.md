# Pricing Strategy

## Positioning

"Your AI-built app is costing you revenue. We fix it fast."

**Target market:** SMBs with revenue ($10k-500k+/mo) who built with AI tools and have broken/unreliable apps.
**Not targeting:** Indie hackers, side projects, hobby apps, sub-$500 budget clients.

## Flat Fee Tiers

| Tier | Price | Scope | Turnaround | Time Budget | Effective Rate |
|------|-------|-------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| Triage | $2,500 | Single critical fix | 48 hrs | 2-3 hrs | $830-1,250/hr |
| Stabilize | $5,000-10,000 | Multiple issues, full stabilization | 1-2 weeks | 8-12 hrs | $415-1,250/hr |
| Rebuild Right | $15,000-25,000 | Re-architect broken parts | 2-4 weeks | 25-35 hrs | $430-1,000/hr |

**Minimum engagement:** $2,500. No exceptions.

## Retainer Tiers

| Tier | Price/mo | Hours | Response SLA | Positioning |
|------|----------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Priority Support | $3,000 | 8 hrs | 24hr | "Less than a junior dev's salary" |
| Fractional CTO | $6,000 | 16 hrs | Same-day | Architecture guidance + execution |

- 3-month minimum commitment
- Unused hours don't roll over
- Overage: $250/hr
- Notify client at 80% usage

## The Diagnostic Session (Free Consult)

**Framing:** "Apply for a Diagnostic Session" — not "book a free call"

**Structure (30 min max):**
1. Review repo/app BEFORE the call (15 min prep)
2. Show up with specific observations
3. Assess complexity, quote a tier
4. End with recommendation + price

**Qualifying via intake form:**
- Company name
- Monthly revenue range
- Active user count
- What's broken (symptoms)
- Urgency level

## Scope Creep Protection

1. **Written scope doc** for every engagement above Triage (3 paragraphs max)
2. **Discovery tax:** If scope is bigger than assessed, stop and communicate. Offer partial fix at original price or upgrade to next tier
3. **One round of revisions** included. Additional = new engagement
4. **"Can you also..."** = new quote, always

## Payment Terms

- 50% upfront for Triage & Stabilize
- 50% up / 25% midpoint / 25% delivery for Rebuild Right
- Retainers: monthly on the 1st, net-7
- Payment via Stripe

## When to Say No

**Hard no:**
- Budget under $2,500
- Can't articulate what's broken
- Fired 3+ developers (problem is the client)
- Regulated data without compliance in place
- "Build me a whole new app" (different business)

**Soft no (price in risk):**
- "I need this tomorrow" → 50-100% rush premium
- Exotic tech stack → assess if worth learning time
- No written agreement → never start work

## Revenue Targets

**Month 6 conservative:**
- 2 Triage: $5,000
- 1 Stabilize: $7,500
- 0.5 Rebuilds: $10,000
- 2 retainers: $6,000
- **Total: ~$28,500/mo**

**Capacity:** ~1,000-1,200 billable hrs/year. Premium pricing means fewer clients, less coordination overhead, more focus per engagement.

## Premium Signals

- "Apply" not "book" — exclusivity framing
- Named packages (Triage/Stabilize/Rebuild Right)
- Show cost of inaction: "2 hrs downtime/week at $500k/yr = $19k lost"
- Invoice from LLC
- Intake form qualifies before call happens
- Case studies > blog posts
